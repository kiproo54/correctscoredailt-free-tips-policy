Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Correct Scores - Daily Tips handles information when you use the app. We aim to keep the service straightforward and respect your privacy.

01

About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Correct Scores - Daily Tips. The app provides football prediction content, including correct-score tips and related match information.

The app is an informational service. Predictions are not guarantees of match outcomes, and the app does not operate a bookmaker or accept bets.

02

Information handled by the app

The app does not ask you to create an account to browse its content. Depending on the features you use and how the app is configured, the following information may be processed:

  • App and device information: Basic technical details or diagnostic information needed to keep the app working reliably, if the relevant platform services are enabled.
  • Football content: Fixtures, prediction tips, results, and app configuration data retrieved from the service that supplies the content.
  • Notifications: If notifications are supported and you enable them, a device notification token may be processed to deliver app alerts.
  • Purchases: If paid features or subscriptions are offered in the app, Google Play handles payment processing. The app publisher does not receive your full bank-card details through Google Play Billing.

We do not use the app to collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or messages unless a future feature clearly requests such access and explains why it is needed.

03

How information is used

Information described above may be used to:

  • Load football fixtures, prediction tips, and match results.
  • Deliver notifications if you have enabled them.
  • Maintain app functionality, diagnose errors, and improve reliability.
  • Verify access to paid features, if such features are available.
  • Respond to support requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not display advertisements in Correct Scores - Daily Tips. The app does not use AdMob or another advertising network to serve ads.

04

Third-party services

The app may rely on third-party services to operate, such as:

  • Firebase or other hosting services: To retrieve prediction content, store app configuration, or deliver notifications, if enabled in the current app version.
  • Google Play services: To distribute the app and process in-app purchases, if purchases are offered.
  • Football data providers: To supply fixtures, scores, team information, or related match data.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies. For Google's privacy information, visit Google Privacy Policy.

When you choose to open an external service or website, its own terms and privacy policy apply.

05

Data retention and security

Service data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app, maintain records, resolve issues, or meet legal requirements. Retention periods can vary depending on the service provider and type of data.

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to help protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06

Your choices and rights

You can turn off app notifications through your Android device settings, where notifications are available. You can stop using the app at any time by uninstalling it.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held about you. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

07

Children and age guidance

The app is intended for an adult audience, particularly because football prediction content may relate to betting. You must comply with the legal age requirements in your country. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

Predictions are for informational and entertainment purposes only. If you choose to bet, do so responsibly and only where it is legal.

08

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its services, or applicable requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made. Please review this page occasionally.
